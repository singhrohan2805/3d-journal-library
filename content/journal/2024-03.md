# March — Spring Approaches

The first crocus appeared today. A small purple defiance against the lingering grey. I stood in the garden for ten minutes just looking at it, feeling absurdly proud, as if I had personally arranged its arrival.

## On Creative Work

I've been thinking a lot about the creative process. How it's less like building a house and more like archaeology — the work already exists somewhere beneath the surface, and your job is to carefully brush away the dirt without breaking anything.

Started a new project this month. Can't say much about it yet — speaking about unfinished work feels like opening the oven door too early. But it involves maps, memory, and the way places hold echoes of everyone who's ever passed through them.

## Walking as Thinking

My best ideas come when I'm walking. There's something about the rhythm of footsteps that loosens the knots in my thinking. I've started taking a different route each day, letting the city surprise me.

Today I found:
- A wall covered entirely in ivy, with a single red door
- A café where the barista was reading Borges between orders
- A street musician playing Debussy on a battered violin

## End of Quarter

Three months into the year. My intentions are holding, mostly. I've missed a few writing days but I'm not beating myself up about it. The point isn't perfection. The point is direction.
